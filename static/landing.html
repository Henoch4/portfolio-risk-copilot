Philosophy

AuditTrail Trader is not a trading bot, a signal service, or another prediction platform.

It pairs three independent signal strategies with a risk gate the AI cannot touch, so a confident model still cannot exceed the limits a human set for it.

Every approved decision is signed and written onchain before the order reaches OKX. If the write fails, the trade does not happen. There is nothing to reconstruct after a bad session.

You can read the same ledger the agent trades against. The point is to make the gap between what the AI decided and what it was allowed to do disappear.
