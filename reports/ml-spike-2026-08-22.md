## Verdict

NO-GO at this configuration. The model finds a faint but real directional
edge (52.5% OOS 4h-direction accuracy vs a 48.4% majority-class baseline),
but it is nowhere near enough to pay 5+3 bps per side at the trading
frequency the 0.55/0.45 bands produce (2,119 position changes, 26%
exposure, ~-90% net over 2y). The honest reading: not "no signal exists",
but "obvious features + default bands + 1h churn cannot clear the gate".
Any follow-up must either raise the conviction bar enough to cut trade
count by ~10x, or find features with materially more edge than returns/
RSI/z-score/volume/funding/hour-of-day. Consistent with the existing
rule-based signals' result in validation-gate-2026-08-22.md.
